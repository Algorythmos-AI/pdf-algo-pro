Internal build of PDF Algo Pro (Staging). Use test documents, not real ones. Please try:

New in this build
- New now: select a shape, stamp, signature or text box and drag it to the top or bottom of the screen, then hold. The page scrolls with it, faster nearer the edge.
- Copy, Cut or Duplicate a stamp, shape or drawing from its bar, then tap where each copy goes; Done ends it. A stamp chosen from Markup > Stamp goes where you tap.
- Edit: paste a name copied from a web page into a line. It changes. If a change can't be saved safely, the app puts that line back, saves the rest and says so.
- Recognise text while filling in a form: what you typed is kept.
- Settings > Require Face ID on a phone with no passcode: the app turns it off and says why, instead of locking you out.
- Plans: when the trial turns into a subscription, Pro stays on.

First run and plans
- Every new build opens on the introduction by itself, then the plans. Nothing to reinstall.
- On the plans, tap Weekly then Yearly: the price line and the button follow.
- Start the yearly trial (nothing is charged in TestFlight): Apple's sheet, then "Welcome to Pro". Also try closing Apple's sheet, and Restore purchases.
- If the plans say this Apple Account already has Pro, that is correct: tap Continue.

Edit text
- Change a line, tap Done, close and reopen: it is still changed. Share it to another PDF app: the new words are there.
- Undo and Redo while editing. Try a much longer replacement: it fits, or the app says why not and changes nothing.
- Open a scan and tap Edit: the app says the page is images and offers Recognise text.

Your file is safe
- Edit a document, save twice, then More > Version history: restore the first version.
- Make an edit and swipe the app away at once. Reopen: the document opens, old or new, never damaged.

Everyday tools
- More: organise pages, reduce size, set or remove a password, flatten, save pages as images.
- Markup: stamp a page, drag a shape to move it, pinch a signature to resize it. Each is one Undo.
- Tap a web link in a PDF: the full address shows before anything opens.

Library and scanning
- Select several documents, then merge, share or delete them. Drag one into Mail or Files.
- Scan two pages: rotate, reorder and name them before saving.

Assistant and privacy
- Ask a question, then a follow-up such as "When is it due?". Both answers cite pages.
- Settings > Require Face ID: the app switcher shows no document.
- Airplane mode: everything above still works.

Known issues
- A drag begun straight after a tap may not move the page. Lift your finger, wait a moment and drag again.
- A PDF renamed in the Files app comes back without its tags, favourite and reading position.

Send feedback
- Take a screenshot in the app, open it, tap the X or Done at the top, then Share Beta Feedback, and say what happened.
- Or open the TestFlight app, tap PDF Algo β, then Send Beta Feedback.
- Or in the app: Settings > Report a problem.
