PDF Algo Pro 1.0 release candidate. This is the build we plan to send to the App Store. Use test documents, not real ones. Please try:

Start as a new customer
- Install fresh. Go through the three introduction pages, then the plans. Tap Close: nothing is sold to you and the app opens.
- On Home, tap Try a sample. Everything below works on that document.
- Settings > Subscription > See plans: tap Weekly then Yearly. The price line and the button follow.
- Start the yearly trial (TestFlight charges nothing): Apple's sheet, then Welcome to Pro. Turn on the reminder if you like.
- Delete the app, install it again, then Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases: Pro comes back.

Read, mark up and sign
- Find a word, highlight a sentence, draw, add a note, a stamp and a shape. Each is one Undo.
- Markup > Signature: sign, then pinch to resize and drag to move.
- Tap a web link in a PDF: the full address shows before anything opens.

Pages and files
- More > Pages: rotate, reorder and delete pages.
- More > Export and protect: reduce the size, add then remove a password, share a flattened copy.
- Edit a document, save twice, then More > Version history: restore the first version.
- In All documents, tap Select, choose two documents and merge them. Drag one into Mail or Files.

Scan
- Scan two pages: rotate, reorder and name them before saving. Then search for a word from the scan.

Assistant and privacy
- Ask > Summarise, then Ask a question and a follow-up such as "When is it due?". The answers cite pages.
- Ask > Explain contract: the result says it is not legal advice.
- On a phone without Apple Intelligence, Ask says why and the rest still works.
- Settings > Require Face ID: the app switcher shows no document.
- Airplane mode: everything above still works, except buying and restoring.

Known issues
- A drag begun straight after a tap may not move the page. Lift your finger, wait a moment and drag again.
- Documents recognised before this build keep misplaced text. Recognise text again to correct them.

Send feedback
- Take a screenshot in the app, open it, tap the X or Done at the top, then Share Beta Feedback, and say what happened.
- Or open the TestFlight app, tap PDF Algo Pro, then Send Beta Feedback.
- Or in the app: Settings > Report a problem.
