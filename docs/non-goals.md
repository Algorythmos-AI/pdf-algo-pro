# Non-goals

What PDF Algo Pro is not, and will not become without a deliberate decision. Non-goals protect the
roadmap from drift: a request that falls under one is declined, or it reopens the non-goal through
the decision register with evidence. Each non-goal says why, and what would make us revisit it.

Owner: Product · Reviewed: each milestone

## PDF Algo Pro is not

| Non-goal | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|
| **A full Adobe Acrobat replacement** | Acrobat's breadth (preflight, print production, enterprise administration) serves professional publishing and IT, not our users; chasing parity would bury the product's edge | Never as a goal; individual capabilities are judged on their own |
| **A desktop publishing or layout tool** | Creating complex layouts from scratch is a different product (Pages, InDesign, Affinity) | Users repeatedly need to author, not edit, documents |
| **A document management system** | Versioned repositories, retention policies, workflows and permissions for organisations are enterprise software | A team or business tier is validated with paying customers |
| **A cloud storage provider** | Users already have iCloud Drive and their own providers; storing documents on our servers contradicts the privacy principle | Never |
| **A CRM or business workflow tool** | Contacts, pipelines and approvals belong in dedicated tools; we integrate through Files, Share and Shortcuts instead | Never |
| **A real-time collaboration suite (V1)** | Co-editing needs servers, identity and conflict resolution, all at odds with the offline, on-device V1 | After V2, if sharing and review become the most requested gap |
| **A DocuSign-class e-signature platform** | Sending documents for others to sign, audit trails and legal envelopes are a regulated service; we offer signing your own documents | Validated demand plus a partner rather than a build |
| **An Android, Windows or web app (V1)** | The moat is native Apple quality; cross-platform would dilute it | The Apple product is established and a platform decision is made on evidence |
| **A legal, medical or financial adviser** | "Analyse Contract" and similar features explain documents; they never give advice, and say so | Never |
| **An advertising-funded app** | Ads and tracking contradict the privacy principle | Never |

## Using this list

- New feature proposals confirm, in the feature template, that they are not a non-goal.
- A non-goal changes only through an entry in the [decision register](decision-register.md) with
  the evidence that triggered the revisit.
