# Product Vision

PDF Algo Pro is the PDF app that understands your documents without taking them from you. It
covers the everyday PDF jobs (read, annotate, sign, edit, organise, convert, scan) and adds
intelligence that is private by default, native to Apple and works offline.

## Seven pillars

PIL-1 Document Reading · PIL-2 Document Editing · PIL-3 OCR & Scanning · PIL-4 AI Document
Intelligence · PIL-5 Privacy · PIL-6 Offline Capability · PIL-7 Native Apple Experience. Every
requirement and roadmap item maps to at least one pillar.

## Why switch, pay and stay

- **Switch:** documents get smarter without leaving the device, and no account is needed.
- **Pay:** editing, redaction, conversion and deeper intelligence in one honest subscription, after
  value has been shown for free.
- **Stay:** a private library that can be searched and questioned, improving every release.

Each is a hypothesis with a test.
