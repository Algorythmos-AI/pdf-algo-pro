# ADRs

Architecture decision records explain why the product is built the way it is. Each has a status,
context, decision, consequences and alternatives. They are append-only: a changed decision gets a
new ADR that supersedes the old one.

The full list, with status, is in the [ADR index](../adr/README.md). Highlights:

- [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-platform-floor-and-swift-6.md): iOS 27 and Swift 6
- [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-pdf-sdk-boundary-and-vendor-selection.md): commercial PDF SDK behind our boundary (Proposed)
- [ADR-0009](../adr/0009-tiered-ai-and-consent.md): tiered intelligence with consent
- [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-two-branch-model.md): `integration` and `main`
- [ADR-0022](../adr/0022-apple-first-capability-baseline.md): Apple-first capability baseline

Every decision, including product and process ones, is also listed in the
[decision register](../decision-register.md).
